Reference data for the **Khmer (Cambodia)** locale — endonym **ខ្មែរ (កម្ពុជា)**, written in the Khmer script, left-to-right (LTR).

- **BCP 47 tag:** `km-KH`

## Translating into this locale

Apply [[locale-km-001]] with the conventions of Cambodia.

- **Calendar:** the Gregorian calendar is used in business; the Khmer solar calendar and Buddhist Era year (2569 is 2026) are used in official documents and festivals (Khmer New Year in April).
- **Currency:** Cambodian riel (៛), with the US dollar widely used for prices; show the currency the product charges.
- **Names and titles:** family name first, then given name; titles like លោក (Mr.), លោកស្រី (Mrs.), and អ្នកស្រី are used.
- **Tone:** polite and respectful, with careful treatment of religion and the monarchy.

## Common pitfalls

- Showing only the Gregorian year on official forms.
- Using Western name order.
- The general pitfalls in [[locale-km-001]] also apply.
