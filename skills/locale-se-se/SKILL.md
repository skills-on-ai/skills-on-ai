# Locale: Northern Sami (Sweden) (se-SE)

Reference data for the **Northern Sami (Sweden)** locale — endonym **Davvisámegiella (Ruoŧŧa)**, written in the Latin script, left-to-right (LTR).

## Identifiers

- **BCP 47 tag:** `se-SE`
- **ISO 639-1:** `se`
- **ISO 639-3:** `sme`
- **Script:** Latn (Latin)
- **Region:** SE (Sweden)
- **Text direction:** ltr — left-to-right (LTR)

## Speakers (approximate)

- **L1 (native):** 5,000
- **L2 (additional):** 2,000
- **L1+L2 (total):** 7,000

## Pluralization

CLDR plural categories for this locale: **one**, **two**, **other** (3 forms).

- **one** — `n = 1` (e.g. 1)
- **two** — `n = 2` (e.g. 2)
- **other** — everything else (e.g. 0, 3~17, 100, 1000, …)

See [[unicode-messageformat]] for writing plural messages using these categories.

## Translating into this locale

Apply [[locale-se-fi]] to Northern Sámi in Sweden, where Sámi is one of five national minority languages, with local rights in the Sámi administrative area.

- **Context:** Sámi is used in education (Sámi schools), media (Sámi Radio), and public services in the administrative area.
- **Formats:** Swedish krona (`kr`); decimal comma; dates with Sámi month names; quotation marks `”…”`; 24-hour clock.
- **Terminology:** use Giellagáldu terms; Swedish administrative terms may be glossed in parentheses.

## Common pitfalls

- Writing Sámi in Swedish word order.
- Dropping Sámi letters from names.
- The general pitfalls in [[locale-se-fi]] also apply.
