# Powerlifting Fitness

Powerlifting is a strength-training discipline built around three
lifts — the squat, bench press, and deadlift — pursued either as a
competitive sport (with formal meets scored on the heaviest single
successful lift in each) or as a training approach for building
maximal strength.

## Why these three lifts

The squat, bench press, and deadlift each test full-body strength
through a different fundamental movement pattern (a lower-body squat
pattern, a horizontal press, and a hip-hinge pull), and together
recruit most of the body's major muscle groups — making them an
efficient foundation even for lifters with no competitive interest.

## Form fundamentals

Because powerlifting focuses on maximal loads, technique matters
enormously — the same movement done with good versus poor form can
mean the difference between a safe, effective lift and a serious
injury. Each lift has specific technical points (bracing, bar path,
depth/lockout requirements) that are worth learning carefully,
ideally with knowledgeable coaching or video review, before adding
significant weight.

## Programming for powerlifting

Powerlifting programs typically periodize training — cycling through
phases of higher volume/lower intensity and lower volume/higher
intensity — building toward peak strength for a specific target
(a meet, or simply a new personal best), rather than training at
maximal effort year-round.

## Common pitfalls

- **Chasing heavier weight before mastering technique** — this is one
  of the most common causes of injury in powerlifting-style training;
  technique should be solid before significant load is added.
- **Skipping a structured program** — random, unplanned training
  without progression or periodization tends to plateau much sooner
  than a structured program.
- **Neglecting recovery between heavy sessions** — maximal-effort
  training is highly taxing on the nervous system and connective
  tissue; inadequate recovery undermines both performance and safety.
- **Training all three lifts with equal heavy frequency without
  planning** — the three lifts have different recovery demands and
  can interfere with each other if not sequenced thoughtfully within a
  training week.
