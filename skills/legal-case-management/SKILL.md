# Legal Case Management

Legal case (or matter) management is the ongoing organizational
discipline of tracking a legal matter's deadlines, documents,
communications, and status from intake through resolution — the
connective backbone linking most of the specific legal-practice
activities in this collection.

## Core elements

- **Intake and conflict clearance** — opening a new matter properly,
  including [[legal-conflict-of-interest-check]] and
  [[legal-client-engagement-letter]].
- **Deadline tracking** — a reliable system for tracking every
  applicable deadline (court filing dates, discovery deadlines,
  statutes of limitations) across all active matters; missed legal
  deadlines can have severe, sometimes irreversible consequences.
- **Document management** — organizing case documents, correspondence,
  and work product so they're findable and properly preserved (see
  [[legal-litigation-hold]] where applicable).
- **Time and billing** — tracking time spent and billing activity
  against the engagement's fee arrangement.
- **Status communication** — keeping the client and relevant internal
  stakeholders appropriately informed of matter progress.

## Why systematic tracking matters so much

Legal deadlines are frequently strict and unforgiving — missing a
statute of limitations or a filing deadline can permanently bar a claim
or defense, independent of its underlying merits. Systematic case
management (rather than relying on individual memory) is what actually
prevents this kind of catastrophic, unforced error.

## Common pitfalls

- **Relying on individual memory for deadlines** — across multiple
  active matters, this is unreliable and risks a missed deadline with
  potentially irreversible consequences.
- **Inconsistent document organization across matters** — makes it
  hard to locate needed material quickly, especially under time
  pressure (e.g. responding to an urgent discovery request).
- **Poor handoffs between team members** — a matter transferred between
  attorneys or paralegals without a clear status summary risks dropped
  deadlines or lost context.
- **Treating case management as purely administrative** — a
  well-managed matter directly supports better legal outcomes (nothing
  falls through the cracks) and isn't just bookkeeping overhead.
