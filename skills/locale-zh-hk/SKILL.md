Reference data for the **Chinese (Traditional, Hong Kong)** locale — endonym **繁體中文 (香港)**, written in the Han (Traditional) script, left-to-right (LTR).

- **BCP 47 tag:** `zh-HK`
- **ISO 639-1:** `zh`

- **Script:** Hant (Han (Traditional))
- **Region:** HK (Hong Kong)
- **Text direction:** ltr — left-to-right (LTR)
